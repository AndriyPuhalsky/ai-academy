Deliver **two** files from `02-references/`, not one:

- `shortlist.md` — 5–8 references, one line each ("what to take and why"), with a link
  and a screenshot. Fits on one screen. Ends with the concrete decision the owner has to
  make.
- `references.md` — the full analysis, written for agent №3 (the builder).

**Why:** the owner personally approves references before agent №3 is launched, and he
does not read 50 pages. Stated explicitly when task 001 was assigned. He also keeps the
start of every pipeline step under his own control, so the shortlist is a decision
document, not a summary.

**How to apply:** write the shortlist *last*, after the full report, and let it name a
recommendation plus the reason — not just a neutral list. Anything the owner must choose
with his eyes (e.g. light vs dark Google button) needs a real screenshot on the real
background colour, not a description.
